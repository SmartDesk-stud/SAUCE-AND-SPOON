**Prepared by:** Morenikeji, Project Manager
**Related document:** project-charter.md (v1.6)

## 1. How to read this analysis

A goal is SMART when it is **S**pecific, **M**easurable, **A**ttainable, **R**elevant, and **T**ime-bound.

- ✅ = meets the criterion
- ⚠️ = needs more detail
- **Bold bracketed text** in the "Original goal" column marks the parts that need more detail

## 4. Step 3: Five goals rewritten to be SMART

I chose the goals that matter most to the sponsor and had the biggest gaps: goals 1, 3, 4, 5, and 9. Items marked **TBD** are details we still need, as the activity allows.

### SMART Goal 1: Table turn time (original goal 1)

> **Reduce the average table turn time in the bar sections of Sauce & Spoon North and Downtown from about 80 minutes to about 50 minutes (a 30-minute reduction) by June 30, by having guests order and pay at tabletop tablets. Turn time will be measured from host software as the average time from seating to table ready for the next party, compared with the baseline captured in the four weeks before launch (baseline: TBD).**

| | How the goal meets the criterion |
|---|---|
| **Specific** | Names the area (bar sections), the two locations, and how the change is achieved (tabletop ordering and payment) |
| **Measurable** | 30 minutes faster, using a defined metric and data source |
| **Attainable** | Ambitious (about 37% faster); to be checked against baseline data and vendor evidence |
| **Relevant** | Supports the under-an-hour dining OKR, more guests per night, and the sponsor's top priority |
| **Time-bound** | Target met by June 30, with a baseline period before launch |

### SMART Goal 2: Appetizer sales (original goal 3)

> **Increase appetizer revenue per guest in the bar sections by 10% at North and 20% at Downtown by June 30 (about 15% on average), compared with the pre-launch baseline (TBD), by showing appetizers and specialty drinks on the tablet menu and offering coupons. The share of parties ordering appetizers (North: about 2/3 after 4pm; Downtown: about 20% at dinner) will be tracked as a supporting metric.**

| | How the goal meets the criterion |
|---|---|
| **Specific** | Defines "sales" as appetizer revenue per guest (so a rise in guest count isn't counted twice) and sets a target for each location |
| **Measurable** | 10% and 20%, from POS data, with a supporting metric |
| **Attainable** | Targets reflect each location's starting point: North already has a high baseline, so its target is lower. Gilly's concern is noted. |
| **Relevant** | Supports the company-wide goal of increasing product mix |
| **Time-bound** | By June 30, measured monthly from April |

### SMART Goal 3: Average check (original goal 4)

> **Raise the average check in the bar sections at both locations from the current baseline (company-wide $65; bar baseline: TBD) to at least $75 by June 30, by promoting appetizers, specialty drinks, and coupons on the tablet. Average check will be taken from POS reports each month (monthly checkpoints for April and May: TBD), and coupon redemption rate will be tracked as a supporting metric (target: TBD%).**

| | How the goal meets the criterion |
|---|---|
| **Specific** | Names the area, the tactics, and the data source |
| **Measurable** | At least $75 (a $10 increase, about 15%), plus a coupon redemption metric |
| **Attainable** | A stretch target; the benefit model in the charter shows it's possible, but interim checkpoints will show early whether we are on track |
| **Relevant** | The sponsor's stated priority; supports profit margins above 8% |
| **Time-bound** | By June 30, with monthly checkpoints |

### SMART Goal 4: Food waste (original goal 5)

> **Reduce food waste and comped meals in the bar sections of both locations by 25% by June 30, compared with a weekly waste and comp baseline kept for four weeks before launch (baseline: TBD dollars per week per location), by sending guest orders and modifications straight to the kitchen. Waste will be measured in dollars per week from the kitchen waste log and POS comp reports, reviewed by each GM.**

| | How the goal meets the criterion |
|---|---|
| **Specific** | Defines what is counted (waste and comps), where, and how the tablets help (direct, recorded orders) |
| **Measurable** | 25% reduction in weekly dollars against a baseline |
| **Attainable** | Fewer ordering errors should mean fewer remakes; confirm with baseline data |
| **Relevant** | Directly supports the operating expense and margin OKRs |
| **Time-bound** | By June 30, with weekly tracking |

### SMART Goal 5: Kitchen capacity (original goal 9)

> **By the April go-live, put in place a BOH capacity monitoring plan that tracks kitchen payroll hours and kitchen ticket times (trigger levels: TBD) weekly at both locations. By July 15 (date: TBD), deliver a staffing recommendation (hire, wait, or reallocate) to Deanna based on April to June data.**

| | How the goal meets the criterion |
|---|---|
| **Specific** | Names what is tracked, how often, where, and what is produced |
| **Measurable** | Weekly tracking against defined trigger levels; one written recommendation |
| **Attainable** | Monitoring is low cost; the hiring decision itself is deliberately left to Deanna |
| **Relevant** | Addresses Alex's and Deanna's concern that the kitchen must keep up, without committing payroll before there is data |
| **Time-bound** | Plan ready by go-live; recommendation by July 15 (TBD) |
